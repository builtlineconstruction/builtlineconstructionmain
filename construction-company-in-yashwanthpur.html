Engineering & Structural Knowledge (FAQs)

1. What are the benefits of PEB (Pre-Engineered Buildings)?

PEB structures are ideal for warehouses and factories in Bangalore because they are quick to assemble, cost-effective, and allow for large clear-spans without internal columns, maximizing usable floor space.

2. Why is 'Camber' important in long-span beams?

Camber is a slight upward curve designed into a beam. It is crucial in industrial construction to offset the natural deflection caused by heavy loads, ensuring the floor remains perfectly level over time.

3. How do you ensure fire safety in commercial buildings?

We integrate fire-rated materials, professional sprinkler systems, and strategic fire exits during the design phase. All our Bangalore commercial builds strictly follow the National Building Code (NBC) for fire safety.
