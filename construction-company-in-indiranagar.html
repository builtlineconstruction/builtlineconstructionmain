Construction Management Insights (FAQs)

1. How do you handle site safety during construction?

We implement strict safety protocols, including mandatory PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) for workers, regular site inspections, and secure scaffolding to ensure a hazard-free environment for both workers and neighbors.

2. What is the significance of the 'Sump Tank' in a building?

An underground sump tank is vital for water storage in Bangalore. We ensure heavy-duty waterproofing and structural RCC design for sumps to prevent leakages and ensure a continuous water supply.

3. Why should I hire an architect and a contractor under one roof?

Using a design-build model ensures better coordination between the plan and the actual construction. This prevents design errors, saves time, and keeps the project budget within the initial estimate.
