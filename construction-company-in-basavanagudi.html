Professional Construction Insights (FAQs)

1. Why is curing essential for concrete structures?

Curing keeps the concrete moist to allow the chemical reaction (hydration) to complete. Proper curing increases the compressive strength and durability of your home, preventing surface cracks and leaks.

2. What is the role of a Plinth Beam in construction?

A plinth beam acts as a tie beam that joins all columns, preventing differential settlement of the building. It also prevents dampness from the soil from reaching the walls of your Bangalore home.

3. Can I make changes to the floor plan after construction starts?

Minor internal changes are possible, but structural changes to beams or columns are not recommended once the RCC work is done. We provide 3D walkthroughs early on to help you finalize your layout before we break ground.
