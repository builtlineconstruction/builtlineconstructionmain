Modern Homeowner Insights (FAQs)

1. What are the benefits of using AAC Blocks over Red Bricks?

AAC (Autoclaved Aerated Concrete) blocks are lightweight, fire-resistant, and provide superior thermal insulation. In the Bangalore climate, they help keep your home cooler and reduce the load on the building's foundation.

2. Is Smart Home automation installed during or after construction?

It is best to plan Smart Home automation during the conduit-laying phase of construction. This allows for hidden wiring and seamless integration of smart lighting, locks, and security systems in your Bangalore home.

3. How do you ensure the building is Earthquake resistant?

We follow Seismic Zone II standards for Bangalore. This involves specific steel reinforcement detailing and the use of ductile ties in columns and beams to ensure the structure can absorb energy during tremors.
