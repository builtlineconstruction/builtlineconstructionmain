Technical Construction FAQ

1. What is the importance of Rainwater Harvesting in new builds?

In Bangalore, rainwater harvesting is mandatory for most plots. It helps in groundwater recharge and provides an alternative water source, making your home more self-sufficient and environmentally responsible.

2. How do you prevent dampness in walls during construction?

We use a Damp Proof Course (DPC) at the plinth level and high-quality waterproofing compounds in the plaster. This prevents moisture from rising through the bricks and protects your interior paint and health.

3. What is 'Built-up Area' vs 'Carpet Area'?

Carpet Area is the actual space you can spread a carpet on. Built-up Area includes the carpet area plus the thickness of inner/outer walls and the balcony space. Understanding this helps in accurate construction cost estimation.
