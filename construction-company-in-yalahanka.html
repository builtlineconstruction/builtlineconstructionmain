Building Technical Guide (FAQs)

1. What is the role of 'Structural Design' in a home?

Structural design is the backbone of your building. It involves calculating the load-bearing capacity of columns, beams, and foundations to ensure the house is safe against gravity, wind, and seismic forces.

2. How does 'Pre-Construction' service help the owner?

Pre-construction involves soil testing, plan approvals, and detailed cost estimation. This stage helps identify potential challenges before digging begins, saving you time and unexpected costs in Bangalore.

3. Why should I use Solar-Ready designs for my roof?

Designing a solar-ready roof ensures that your electrical conduits and structural slabs are ready to hold solar panels. This is a smart way to future-proof your home and reduce long-term electricity bills.
