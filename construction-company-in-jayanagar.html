Building Construction Guide (FAQs)

1. What are the common causes of cracks in new buildings?

Cracks can occur due to foundation settlement, thermal expansion, or poor curing of concrete. Using high-quality TMT steel and ensuring proper 21-day curing periods significantly reduces this risk.

2. How do I choose the right grade of cement for my house?

For residential builds in Bangalore, we generally recommend OPC 53 grade for structural RCC work and PPC cement for brickwork and plastering to prevent shrinkage cracks.

3. Is it possible to build a house during the monsoon season?

Yes, but it requires proper site drainage and protective covering for materials like cement and sand. We plan our schedules to ensure RCC work is ideally done during dry windows for maximum strength.
