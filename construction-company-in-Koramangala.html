Essential Construction Knowledge (FAQs)

1. What is the difference between Load-Bearing and Framed Structures?

A load-bearing structure uses walls to support the weight, whereas a framed structure (RCC) uses columns and beams. Framed structures are preferred for modern Bangalore buildings as they offer more flexibility in floor plans.

2. Why is Soil Testing important before construction?

Soil testing determines the load-bearing capacity of the ground. It helps engineers design the right foundation type (isolated, raft, or pile) to prevent future cracks or structural sinking.

3. How can I make my new home more energy-efficient?

We recommend rainwater harvesting, solar panel integration, and using AAC blocks for better thermal insulation, helping you reduce utility costs in the long run.
